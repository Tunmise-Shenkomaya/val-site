Greetings, Noble Lady!

Through these scrolls I write to thee, a humble message of admiration...

My Tomtom...

A humble knight embarks upon a quest of the heart, and thou art the treasure he seeks. This isn’t one of those questions people ask just because the moment feels right, or because everyone else seems to be asking it. This is me choosing my words carefully, because you matter that much. You’ve been there — not only in the easy days, but in the quiet ones, the heavy ones, the days when illness tried to slow me down and the days when happiness felt almost too big to hold alone. You stayed. Always. Your beauty isn’t just something to look at — it’s something you feel. In the way you speak, in the way your presence fills a room (more like our chats, but still), in the way your personality shines without trying. Your humor is effortless, your gratitude sincere — sometimes so sincere it catches me off guard. You notice the smallest gestures, and somehow make them feel important. And your smile… Your smile does something to me. It warms me. Grounds me. It reminds me why this isn’t random, why this isn’t casual, why this matters. So before you turn the page, know that what awaits you there comes from intention, not impulse. And I cannot wait to see the smile this little quest brings to your face.

Will you be my Val? 💘
